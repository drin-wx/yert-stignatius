What we stand for

One org. Two team.
Two missions.

Y.E.R.T. connects environmental stewardship with emergency response so students can build skills, confidence, discipline, and a genuine culture of service.

Environment Team

Promote responsible environmental practices, campus cleanliness, conservation, awareness campaigns, and student-led green projects.

Explore Team

Rescue Team

Develop preparedness through first-aid learning, emergency drills, basic rescue awareness, safety support, and responsible response.

Why Y.E.R.T.

Youth leadership with a purpose.

Our goal is not simply to create a team. It is to build students who understand that protecting the environment and helping people are connected responsibilities.

Learn basic first aid and emergency preparedness principles.
Join environmental activities and campus awareness projects.
Practice teamwork, communication, discipline, and leadership.
Serve within safe, supervised, and school-approved activities.
